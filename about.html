About Essential Arabic Learning

Essential Arabic Learning was founded with one clear purpose: to make the Arabic language, and the words of the Qur'an, accessible to everyone — starting from the very first letter. We specialize in teaching beginners and children, building a strong and joyful foundation in Arabic reading, writing, and Qur'anic recitation. It's the kind of foundation that makes everything that comes after so much easier.

Our courses — Arabic Beginner Course, Quranic Arabic Course, and Nazrat ul Quran + Basic Education — are designed step by step, so that a student with zero prior knowledge can progress with confidence, at their own pace.

Our Mission

Our mission is to open the door to the Arabic language for young learners and absolute beginners, using teaching methods that are patient, encouraging, and rooted in traditional Islamic learning — so that every student, no matter where they start, can read, understand, and connect with the Qur'an with confidence.

Meet the Founder

Hasnat Asif is an Islamic Researcher and the founder of Essential Arabic Learning. He graduated in Islamic Studies from GIFT University, Gujranwala, where his passion for the Arabic language first took root.

Driven by a keen interest in Arabic learning and teaching, Hasnat has dedicated himself to making the language accessible to students of all ages. His own proficiency in Arabic has been formally assessed and certified through the Hamza Test (اختبار همزة) — reflecting the same standard of learning he brings to every student at Essential Arabic Learning.

Hasnat's research work includes his thesis, "Robert Spencer's Concept of Jihad: An Analytical and Critical Study," which critically examines Robert Spencer's writings and interpretations of Jihad, evaluating his claims against classical Islamic scholarship and source texts. You can read & download the full thesis on his ResearchGate profile.
